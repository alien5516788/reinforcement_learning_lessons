Stochastic Policy
    - Outputs a probability

      π(a | s)

ε-greedy Policy
    - Special type of stochastic policy
    - It assigns highest probability to best action and devide remaining ε probability to among of the actions

      π(s, a) -> {
                    1 - ε + (ε / |A(s)|) ,  if a == arg max_a Q(s, a)
                    ε / |A(s)| ,            if a != arg max_a Q(s, a)
                 }

Deterministic Policy
    - Outputs an action
    - Each state has only one possible action with probability of 1

      μ(s) or π(s)

Greedy Policy
    - Special type of deterministic policy
    - Always chooses the best action

      π(s) -> arg max_a Q(s, a)
